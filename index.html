05 — FAQ

Questions clients
ask before hiring.

Still unsure?
Send a message or ask on WhatsApp.

How do we start a project?

Send a short brief on WhatsApp or form: what the video is for, a reference you like & deadline. You'll get a clear quote, timeline and the best way to send footage.

What is your typical turnaround?

Short-form reels take 24–72 hours, commercials 3–7 days and event films about a week — with rush 48-hour delivery available for launches and campaigns.

How many revisions are included?

Every project includes structured revisions with time-stamped feedback so changes are fast and precise — the goal is simple: you approve a cut you are proud to publish.

How do I send large footage files?

Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer or Frame.io — and for 6K/RAW projects, lightweight proxies keep editing fast.

Do you work with clients outside Egypt?

Yes — most clients are remote across KSA, UAE & Canada, with communication in Arabic or English planned around your time zone.

Which file formats do you deliver?

H.264/H.265 for social and ProRes 422 HQ for broadcast, framed in 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:5 — plus SRT subtitles on request.

How is payment handled?

Through Upwork, Mostaql, or directly by agreement — with larger projects split into milestones paid as work is approved.
